FEDERAL OFFICIALS CANNOT BE RECALLED — BUT THEY CAN BE REMOVED

The U.S. Constitution provides no recall mechanism for federal officials. Courts have consistently ruled that state recall laws do not apply to Congress members. HOW TO PUNCH FROM EVERY DIRECTION SIMULTANEOUSLY

Step 1 — Document Everything
Gather all public records: FEC filings, financial disclosures, voting records, lobbying reports, court records. Everything is public. Use PACER, FEC.gov, OpenSecrets, and your state's campaign finance portal.
Step 2 — File Simultaneously
File FEC complaint AND Ethics Committee complaint AND DOJ referral on the same day. Multiple concurrent investigations create pressure from every direction. They can't make all of them disappear.
Step 3 — Make It Public
File complaints then publish them. Send to local press. Post the receipts. Public pressure combined with legal proceedings is exponentially more effective than either alone. Sunlight is the best disinfectant.
Step 4 — Organize Locally
Connect with others in the district. Coordinate recall petitions (state officials), primary challenges, and donor pressure campaigns simultaneously. The system can suppress one person. It cannot suppress an organized community filing from every legal angle.
