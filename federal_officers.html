FEDERAL OFFICIALS CANNOT BE RECALLED — BUT THEY CAN BE REMOVED

The U.S. Constitution provides no recall mechanism for federal officials. Courts have ruled state recall laws do not apply to Congress members. However the Constitution and federal law provide 12 distinct legal mechanisms to remove, investigate, prosecute, or sanction federal officials. Every tool below is legal, documented, and has successfully been used. File simultaneously. Make it public. Don't stop.
