FEDERAL OFFICIALS CANNOT BE RECALLED — BUT THEY CAN BE REMOVED

The U.S. Constitution provides no recall mechanism for federal officials. Courts have ruled state recall laws do not apply to Congress members. However the Constitution and federal law provide 12 distinct legal mechanisms to remove, investigate, prosecute, or sanction federal officials. Every tool below is legal, documented, and has successfully been used. File simultaneously. Make it public. Don't stop.

HOW TO PUNCH FROM EVERY DIRECTION SIMULTANEOUSLY

1
Document Everything First
Pull all public records before filing anything. FEC filings at fec.gov/data, financial disclosures at efts.house.gov, voting records at congress.gov, lobbying reports at lda.senate.gov, OpenSecrets donor data at opensecrets.org. Build the case before you file.
2
File Simultaneously — Same Day
File FEC complaint AND Ethics Committee complaint AND DOJ tip on the same day. Multiple concurrent investigations create pressure from every direction. File SEC whistleblower report if financial crimes involved. File IRS Form 211 if tax fraud involved. They cannot make all of them disappear simultaneously.
3
Publish Everything You Filed
After filing, publish the complaint documents. Send to local press, national press, and post publicly. The FEC complaint is public record. The Ethics complaint is public record. "I have filed a formal complaint with the House Ethics Committee and the FEC regarding..." — that sentence alone creates pressure. Sunlight does what courts can't.
4
Track the STOCK Act Yourself
Check efts.house.gov monthly. Any trade not reported within 45 days is a violation. Document it. File it with the Ethics Committee. This is one of the easiest provable violations — the date is public record, the trade is public record, the deadline is the law.
5
Use FARA as the Weapon It Is
Foreign Agent Registration Act violations are federal felonies. Check FARA registrations at efts.fara.gov. If a politician or their staff received money from foreign interests without registering — that's a crime. Report to DOJ National Security Division at justice.gov/nsd-fara.
6
Coordinate and Organize
One complaint from one person is easy to ignore. One hundred coordinated complaints filed on the same day from organized constituents is impossible to ignore. Connect with others in the district. Split the work — one person files FEC, one files Ethics, one files DOJ, one handles press. The system is built to suppress individuals. It cannot suppress an organized coordinated effort.
